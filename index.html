Who decides

The board, and how it has changed

The Monetary Policy Board was created on 1 March 2025 and seats nine: the Governor, the Deputy Governor, the Secretary to the Treasury, and six external members appointed by the Treasurer. Who holds those seats is public. How any of them votes is not.

      This section records membership only. The vote record above is unattributed — the RBA publishes the count of votes for and against, never who voted which way — so nothing here connects a name to a decision, and a change of membership should not be read as an explanation of any vote.

      The votes are unattributed

      From July 2025 the RBA publishes only the count of votes for and against the decision taken — for example “6 in favour, 3 against.” Who voted which way is not disclosed, so this record never names members.

      The board

      The Monetary Policy Board has nine members — the Governor, the Deputy Governor, the Secretary to the Treasury, and six external members. It meets about eight times a year and decides by majority of those present.
